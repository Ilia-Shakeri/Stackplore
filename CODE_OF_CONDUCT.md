# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the Stackplore community a respectful, safe, and welcoming experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, ability, appearance, nationality, or beliefs.

We act in ways that support an open, healthy, and productive community.

## Expected behavior

Examples of positive behavior include:

- Showing empathy, patience, and respect
- Giving and receiving constructive technical feedback
- Focusing disagreement on ideas, evidence, and project needs
- Respecting different experience levels and communication styles
- Accepting responsibility, apologizing when appropriate, and learning from mistakes
- Protecting the privacy and safety of community members
- Putting the long-term health of the project ahead of individual preference

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment, intimidation, threats, or discriminatory language
- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted attention
- Trolling, insults, personal attacks, or sustained disruption
- Publishing or threatening to publish another person's private information
- Deliberately exposing credentials, private infrastructure data, or other sensitive material
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern
- Conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces and whenever someone is publicly representing the project. Project spaces include repositories, issue trackers, review threads, discussions, events, and other official communication channels.

## Reporting

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior privately through the contact route described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). State clearly that the report concerns community conduct rather than a technical vulnerability.

Do not open a public issue if doing so would expose personal, private, or sensitive information.

If a report concerns every maintainer who can receive the project route, use
the hosting platform's private abuse-reporting tools. The project cannot promise
an independent internal reviewer when no conflict-free maintainer is available.

Maintainers will review reports as promptly and fairly as reasonably possible. They will protect the reporter's privacy and safety to the extent possible, share information only as needed to investigate, and avoid conflicts of interest. A maintainer named in a report will not take part in handling it.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and communications that violate this code. Responses will be proportionate to the nature, impact, and recurrence of the behavior and may include:

1. **Correction:** A private explanation of the problem and the behavior expected.
2. **Warning:** A formal warning with consequences for continued behavior.
3. **Temporary restriction:** A time-limited restriction from project interaction.
4. **Permanent restriction:** Permanent removal from project spaces for severe or repeated violations.

Good-faith reports will not be punished. Knowingly false or retaliatory reports may themselves violate this code.
